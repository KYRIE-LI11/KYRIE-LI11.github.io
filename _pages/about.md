Hi!👋 My name is Hanqian Li(李翰乾). I am a senior undergraduate student majoring in Automation at Shandong University(SDU). Currently, I work as a research assistant at **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)**. In August 2025, I will join the AI Thrust, Information Hub at **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)** to pursue an MPhil degree under the supervision of [Prof. Xuming Hu](https://xuminghu.github.io/). I am honored to collaborate with [Dr. Aiwei Liu](https://exlaw.github.io/) and Dr. Sirui Huang on research projects.

My research interests focus on **trustworthy large language models** and **computer vision**. Currently, I am particularly interested in **text watermark in large language models** and **video watmermark**, and I am conducting in-depth research in these areas.
